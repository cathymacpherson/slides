Our Students Didn't Feel the Same Way

Attitudes and intentions (1–7 scale)
University students (n = 320) Community sample (n = 186)
Who held the negative views?

Students who had never used AI for their mental health. Those who had sat around neutral.

  • Turns out, our student sample didn't exactly feel the same way.
  • When we asked 320 university students the same questions, attitudes and intentions both fell below neutral.
  • And that negativity sat with students who had never used AI for their mental health. Those who had were right around neutral.
